Lead Sources and Health Information

People can be exposed to lead from a variety of environmental sources. Each exposure contributes to the amount of lead in the body. Common exposure sources include dust from old lead paint, contaminated soil, certain imported foods and cookware, and drinking water. Lead can enter drinking water at the point-of-use due to lead-containing fixture components or plumbing corroding.

Children six years old and younger are the most susceptible to the effects of lead. Adults with elevated exposure can have increased risks of heart disease, high blood pressure, kidney damage, or nervous system problems.
